Sentence or paragraph, no dragging

No dragging. Tap any word, then Sentence. Leaf finds the whole sentence around that word and translates it with Google.

  1. Tap any word on the page
  2. Small dictionary popup → tap Sentence
  3. Sentence is shaded on the page; original + Vietnamese shown. Switch to Paragraph for the whole paragraph, shown sentence by sentence
  4. Optional: Use AI for a structure explanation

Sentence replaces the old Explain here button, so the row stays at four buttons.
Tap outside a popup to close it. Dragging across words still translates exactly what you selected.
