IELTS Reading on Leaf

Mockup at Kindle size. Grayscale, discrete screens, big tap targets.

  1. Start: pick Practice (20-min timer, word tools off, checked at the end) or Study (no timer, tap words for dictionary/translation).
  2. Passage ⇄ Questions switch at the top replaces the side-by-side layout. The square dots show answered questions.
  3. TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN: one tap.
  4. One-word blanks: tap the blank, the passage opens, tap the word. No keyboard. (Keyboard stays available under “Type”.)
  5. Results: score, band estimate, and Show in passage shades the sentence that holds the answer.

The passage and questions are original sample text in IELTS style. Real tests would be pasted on the Mac and turned into a Leaf test file.
